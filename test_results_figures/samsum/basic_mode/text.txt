\textbf{\emph{Basic Mode}}

In the SAMSum dataset, using the Basic Mode, we observe that the adaptive optimizers NAdam, AdamW, and Adam reported very similar results across all metrics (see Tables \ref{samsum_test_results_1} and \ref{samsum_test_results_2}). On the other hand, the SGD and SGDM optimizers reported zero values for most metrics, with the exception of Test Loss, where SGDM showed a slight advantage over SGD. However, both still lag significantly behind the performance of the adaptive optimizers.
