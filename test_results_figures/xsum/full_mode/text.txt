\textbf{\emph{Full Mode}}

In the Full Mode, where additional hyperparameters were tuned for each optimizer, we observe an overall improvement in performance across all metrics (see Tables \ref{xsum_test_results_1_full_training} and \ref{cnn_test_results_2_full_training}). Compared to the Basic Mode, all optimizers show enhanced performance. Among the adaptive optimizers, there is no significant difference in performance that favors one over the others. For the SGD and SGDM optimizers, SGDM demonstrates better test loss scores, although both SGD and SGDM report zero values for all other metrics, continuing to lag behind the adaptive optimizers.
